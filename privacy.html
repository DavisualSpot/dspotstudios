Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

We collect as little as possible. This page explains what we see and why.

This website

The site uses no analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of its own. It is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser and pages requested) for security and reliability.

When you buy

Checkout is run by Lemon Squeezy, our Merchant of Record. They collect your name, email, billing country and payment details to process the order, calculate tax and prevent fraud. We never see your full card details. We receive your name, email, country and what you bought, so we can deliver files, give support and keep sales records. See the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy.

When you email us

If you contact us, we use your email and message only to reply to you.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data.
  • We don't send marketing emails unless you've asked us to.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time. Records we must keep for tax or legal reasons are kept only as long as required. Email dspotlab@gmail.com.
