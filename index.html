Questions

Can I use these in client work and ads?

Yes. Use them in videos, ads, films, podcasts, apps, games, websites and social posts, for yourself or for clients, in as many projects as you like, with no royalties.

Can I use them on monetised YouTube, TikTok or Instagram?

Yes. Just don't register the sounds with Content ID or any other rights-claiming system. That's what keeps them free of claims for everyone who buys them.

What can't I do?

You can't resell or share the sounds themselves, put them in another sound pack, template or preset bank, or use them to train AI. The licence isn't exclusive, so the sounds can't be trademarked as your sonic logo. For an exclusive sonic logo, email me for a custom one.

What does "made from maths" mean?

Each sound is generated by code: oscillators, filtered noise, envelopes and synthetic reverb. No recordings, samples or AI models are inside, so there are no hidden third-party rights.

Why 12 keys?

Bells, chimes, stingers, pads, kalimba and log drums come in every key. Pick the one that matches your music and the sound sits in tune with the track, not on top of it.

What format are the files?

WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, stereo, mastered to −1 dBFS peak, named clearly and sorted into folders. They work in Premiere, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut, CapCut and any DAW.

How do I get the files?

Right after payment you get a download link by email. If anything doesn't work, email and I'll sort it out.
