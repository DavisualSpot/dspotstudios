Refund policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Our packs are digital downloads that you can preview before buying (see the previews on our homepage). Because the files can't be returned once downloaded, sales are generally final. We will refund you in these cases:

  • Broken or missing files. If a download is corrupted or incomplete and we can't fix it with a replacement file, you get a full refund.
  • Not as described. If the pack is materially different from its description on this site.
  • Duplicate purchase. If you accidentally bought the same pack twice, or bought a pack and then the bundle that contains it.

How to ask

Email dspotlab@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase with your order number and what went wrong. We reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds are issued through Lemon Squeezy to your original payment method, and your licence for that pack ends once the refund is made.
